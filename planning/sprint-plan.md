# Sprint Plan

## Sprint 1
Goal: working backend foundation.
Deliver: auth, PostgreSQL, migrations, health check.

## Sprint 2
Goal: execution planning.
Deliver: goals, milestones, tasks.

## Sprint 3
Goal: evidence tracking.
Deliver: projects, skills, time logs.

## Sprint 4
Goal: feedback loop.
Deliver: daily and weekly reviews.

## Sprint 5
Goal: visibility.
Deliver: dashboard, analytics, reality checks.

## Sprint 6
Goal: usable MVP.
Deliver: testing, responsive UI, deployment.

Definition of done: feature works, is persisted, handles validation, has basic tests, and is documented.
